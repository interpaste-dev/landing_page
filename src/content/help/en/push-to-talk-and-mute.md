## Desktop app

The desktop app doesn’t have push-to-talk yet — a global push-to-talk key is planned. Until then:

1. **Mute and unmute quickly**

   Press **Ctrl+Shift+M** or click the microphone button in the voice panel. A crossed-out microphone shows that you’re muted.

2. **Turn on noise suppression**

   Open settings (**Ctrl+,**) → **Voice**. Noise suppression and echo cancellation run locally, before your voice is encrypted.

3. **Test your microphone**

   In the same place, use the microphone test: the level bar moves live and you can listen to yourself.

4. **Set input sensitivity**

   Under **Input sensitivity**, the microphone only transmits when you’re louder than the threshold (the vertical line on the level bar) — keyboard clicks and fan noise stay out. **Determine automatically** makes the threshold follow your background noise.

You can switch your microphone or headphones during a call — the call keeps going on the new device.

## Terminal client

1. **Join voice**

   **F7** joins or leaves the voice call in the current conversation. **F8** mutes the microphone.

2. **Turn on push-to-talk**

   Open settings (**Ctrl+,**) and turn on **push-to-talk (toggle mode)**. The default key is **F12**: press it to start talking, press again to stop.

> **Push-to-talk in the terminal client works while its window is focused.** If you play in fullscreen, use the mute key before you switch to the game.
