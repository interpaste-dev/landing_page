When a contact’s device key changes, Komunikator shows a banner in the conversation: **“The key of device … has changed. Compare the number before you send anything important.”** The conversation is marked **Unverified**. You’ll see the same warning if a contact’s key differs from the one the server knows.

Usually there’s an innocent explanation:

- they reinstalled the app or logged in again,
- they added a new device,
- they logged out an old one.

Less often, it means someone is trying to impersonate them. That’s why it’s worth checking before you send anything important.

## What to do

1. **Ask them through another channel**

   Call them or ask in a voice channel whether they changed devices or logged in again.

2. **Compare the safety number**

   Click **Check** in the banner, or **Show number** in the side panel, and compare the number with your friend — in person or over a call. You can also open it from the command palette (**Ctrl+K** → **Safety number**).

3. **Confirm or reject**

   If the numbers match, choose **It matches — verify** (or **Accept new key**). If they don’t, choose **It doesn’t match**.

> **The number doesn’t match and they didn’t change anything?** Don’t send anything sensitive to this contact. Someone may be standing between your devices. Tell your friend through a different channel so they can check their devices in Settings → Devices.

In the terminal client, safety numbers are under **Ctrl+S**. Want to understand them better? Read [Safety numbers explained](/blog/safety-numbers-explained/).
