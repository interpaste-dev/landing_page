When a contact’s key changes, Komunikator shows a warning like **“Zosia’s security key has changed”**, with the previous and the new fingerprint. Usually there’s an innocent explanation:

- they reinstalled the app,
- they added a new device (for example, a new phone),
- they restored their account from a backup.

Less often, it means someone is trying to impersonate them. That’s why it’s worth checking before you send anything important.

## What to do

1. **Pause sending if you’re unsure**

   Tick **“Pause sending to Zosia until verified”** in the warning. Messages won’t go out until you confirm the new key.

2. **Ask them through another channel**

   Call them or ask in a voice channel whether they changed devices or reinstalled the app.

3. **Compare the new number**

   Click **“Compare number”** and compare the safety number in person, or scan the QR code with the app on their phone. If it matches, confirm with **“It matches — verify”**.

> **The number doesn’t match and they didn’t change anything?** Don’t send anything sensitive to this contact and choose **“It doesn’t match”**. Tell them through a different channel so they can check their devices in Settings › Devices & security.
