Your PIN decrypts the **local key store** on this device. It is never sent to the server, so we can’t reset it or tell you what it was.

## Try these first

1. **Use Windows Hello**

   If you turned it on, choose “Use Windows Hello” on the unlock screen instead of typing the PIN.

2. **Check your keyboard layout and Num Lock**

   A different layout or Num Lock turned off is the most common reason a correct PIN is rejected.

## If you really don’t remember it

Choose **“I forgot my PIN”** on the unlock screen. The app will clear the keys stored on this device, and you can then:

- **restore them from a backup** — see [How to restore your account from a backup](/help/restore-account-from-backup/), or
- **log in on a device that still works** and create a new backup there.

> **After 5 wrong attempts** the local keys are wiped automatically. This protects your conversations if someone else is trying to guess your PIN. Your other devices are not affected.

## Why can’t you reset it for me?

Because the PIN never leaves your computer. That’s what keeps the key store safe if your laptop is stolen — and it’s also why a backup phrase is the only way back in.
