The 6-digit PIN locks Komunikator on **this computer**. It is never sent to the server, so we can’t reset it or tell you what it was.

## Wrong attempts

After **5 wrong attempts**, the app makes you wait **30 seconds** before you can try again. Nothing is deleted.

## If you really don’t remember it

1. **Choose “I forgot my PIN”**

   It’s on the unlock screen.

2. **Log out and log in again with your password**

   Logging out removes the account and the history stored on this computer. Your conversations continue — new messages arrive after you log in again.

> **Your other devices are not affected.** They keep their history, and you can add this computer back to your conversations from them.

## Why can’t you reset it for me?

Because the PIN never leaves your computer. That’s what protects the app if someone gets hold of your laptop — and it’s also why the only way back without it is your password.
